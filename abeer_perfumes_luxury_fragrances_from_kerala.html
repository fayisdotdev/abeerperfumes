Centuries of Trade & Essence

Born from the Historic Spice Ports of Malabar

Long before modern perfumery, Arab merchants and European explorers sailed to Kerala's coasts seeking green cardamom, black pepper, and precious sandalwood. Abeer Perfumes carries this regal legacy forward.

We ethically source wild vetiver from River Bharathapuzha, fresh night-blooming jasmine (*Mulla*) from local family farms in Palakkad, and rich aged agarwood from sustainable Assam plantations.

Wayanad Cardamom

Top Note • Warm Spice

Hand-picked green cardamom pods from high-altitude Wayanad slopes. Gives an invigorating, spicy resinous sweetness to top accords.

Palakkad Mulla Jasmine

Heart Note • Indolic Floral

Harvested strictly before dawn when fragrance concentration peaks. Intensely intoxicating, creamy, and romantic floral richness.

Ramacham (Vetiver)

Base Note • Earthy Cool

Traditional cooling roots washed in backwater streams. Offers deep woody, damp earthy notes that calm and ground the senses.
